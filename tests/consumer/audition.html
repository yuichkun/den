THREE LISTENING CHECKPOINTS

01 / The edge

Keep depth at zero. Raise attack, then hold the pad. Does the sound rise smoothly?

02 / The tail

Release the pad. Compare a short and long release. Stop audio should always silence the output.

03 / The motion

Add 0.5 semitones of depth. Move LFO rate slowly. Listen for a steady pitch wobble without a phase jump.
